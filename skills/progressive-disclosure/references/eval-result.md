# Eval result

**Verdict: improve (n=2 trials, 4 arm-runs total)**

The first trial, a baseline framing against a corpus-scale fixture, found
no discrimination — both arms found the right entry via the index and
stopped, at comparable cost. A second trial, adding a competing pull
toward reading exhaustively rather than searching selectively, did
discriminate: the skill-installed arm held to selective reading while the
no-skill arm read substantially more of the corpus than it needed to,
under real Agent-tool cost accounting rather than either arm's own
self-report. Recorded `improve` on the strength of that second trial's
real, mechanically-measured cost divergence, keeping the first trial's
clean non-result as context rather than discarding it.
