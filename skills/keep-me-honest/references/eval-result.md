skill's own named rules — every arm, across both pairs, correctly held

didn't discriminate — both arms independently re-verified the underlying
claim and held their position in the fresh, memory-cut session, with or

this skill's own specific mechanism at this scale, not evidence the
mechanism doesn't matter in general — the check this skill governs
